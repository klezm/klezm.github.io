## Text and footnotes

Ordinary prose is capped at a readable measure regardless of how wide the window
gets. Footnote references get a popover on hover or keyboard focus[^measure],
falling back to a plain anchor jump when JavaScript is unavailable[^nojs].

[^measure]:
    Roughly 65 characters per line. Longer lines make it harder for the
    eye to find the start of the next one.

[^nojs]:
    The popovers are progressive enhancement — the markup is standard GFM
    footnotes and works without any script.

## Math

Inline math like $e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$ sits in the run of text. Display math gets
its own block and scrolls horizontally rather than widening the page:

## Content cards

<ContentCard title="A card with a caption">
  Cards group related content and cap their own prose at the readable measure,
  so a card never produces longer lines than the surrounding text.

  <span slot="caption">The caption sits on a tinted band rather than behind a border.</span>
</ContentCard>

<ContentCard title="Horizontal stacking" stack="horizontal">
  Mobile-first: this stacks vertically by default and only splits into columns
  at 40rem and up.

In a horizontal split each column is already narrower than the readable
measure, so the per-child width cap is lifted — otherwise the columns would
sit visibly short of their tracks.

</ContentCard>
